# RhoQ

RhoQ is a fitness community platform where users can work out
alongside other people remotely.

## Premium

RhoQ Premium costs ₹499 per month.

Premium users get unlimited broadcast hours, unlimited messaging
with other people on the platform, and the ability to share social
links.

## Referral Program

RhoQ users can earn ₹1,000 for every 10 successful conversions.

A successful conversion happens when someone signs up through the
user's referral and subscribes to a paid RhoQ plan.

Users who are referred receive ₹100 off Premium.

## Creator Program

Creators can host live workouts on RhoQ and set a price for people
to join their workouts.

Users who subscribe to a creator's plan automatically receive a
RhoQ Premium subscription.
