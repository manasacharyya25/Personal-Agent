# RhoQ Knowledge Base

## About RhoQ

RhoQ is a fitness community platform designed to make working out feel more social. Users can join workout rooms, see other people working out, share workout content, and participate in a community built around fitness and consistency.

RhoQ is not primarily a platform for hiring personal trainers. The focus is on community, participation, motivation, and discovering other people who are working toward their fitness goals.

Users can create profiles and share workout-related content with the community. They can also join workout rooms and work out alongside other members.

## RhoQ Workout Rooms

Workout rooms allow members to work out alongside other people.

A user can enter a room and see other participants working out. The experience is designed to provide social accountability and motivation while exercising.

RhoQ workout rooms are not traditional video calls. The platform focuses on showing workout activity rather than providing a conventional voice or video conferencing experience.

Users can join a room when they want some company or motivation during their workout.

## Community

RhoQ is built around a fitness community rather than individual coaching.

Members can create posts, share workout videos, interact with other users, and discover people with similar fitness interests.

The community is intended to make fitness more engaging and less isolated.

Users are encouraged to participate regularly, share their progress, and interact with other members.

## Profiles

Every RhoQ user can create a profile.

A profile can contain information relevant to the user's fitness journey. During onboarding, users can provide information such as their weight, height, activity status, fitness goals, and experience level.

Experience levels are divided into three broad categories.

Beginner generally means someone with less than one year of training experience.

Intermediate generally means someone with one to three years of training experience.

Advanced generally means someone with more than three years of training experience.

## Fitness Goals

RhoQ users can identify their fitness goals during onboarding.

Common goals can include building muscle, losing weight, improving general fitness, increasing strength, improving endurance, and maintaining consistency.

The purpose of collecting this information is to help create a more relevant experience for each user.

Fitness information can also help personalize recommendations and workout-related experiences.

## Workout Content

Users can share workout videos and fitness-related posts on RhoQ.

Workout content can be used to document progress, share routines, and interact with other members.

Creators can use RhoQ to build an audience around their fitness content and engage with people who are interested in their workouts.

The platform is intended to provide creators with an additional way to interact with their fitness audience.

## Fitness Creators

RhoQ works with fitness creators who want to build an audience and engage with their followers.

Creators can create posts and participate in workout sessions.

Creators can also invite members of their audience to work out alongside them.

Early creators participating in the RhoQ creator program may receive benefits such as a premium membership and compensation depending on the current collaboration program.

Creator programs and benefits can change over time.

## Creator Revenue Sharing

RhoQ has explored a creator revenue sharing model.

Under this model, users can join workouts hosted by creators and the creator can receive a share of the revenue generated by participating users.

The exact revenue-sharing percentage and creator payment structure may change as the product evolves.

Creator payouts may initially be handled manually rather than through an automated payout system.

## Premium Membership

RhoQ offers a Premium membership.

Premium provides access to additional features and benefits that are not necessarily available to free users.

The current Premium price is ₹499 per month.

Pricing and Premium benefits may change as RhoQ evolves.

Users should be directed to the latest official pricing information when exact current pricing is important.

## Referral Program

RhoQ has a referral program designed to encourage existing users to invite new members.

A successful referral can provide benefits to both the person making the referral and the new user.

The current referral structure provides ₹1,000 for every 10 successful conversions.

A referral can also provide ₹100 off Premium for the referred user.

Referral eligibility depends on the current referral program rules.

Users should check the latest referral terms before relying on a specific reward.

## Getting Started

A new user should first explore the RhoQ platform and understand the available features.

The basic onboarding process includes creating a profile and providing relevant fitness information.

After creating a profile, users can create a few posts and explore the community.

Users can also join live workout rooms to experience the social workout functionality.

The goal of onboarding is to help a new member become comfortable with the platform before participating more actively.

## Creator Onboarding

Creators interested in collaborating with RhoQ can first explore the platform and create their profile.

They can then create posts and join workout rooms.

Once the creator understands the platform, they can discuss the type of content or offering they would like to provide to their audience.

RhoQ may provide early creators with promotional or membership benefits as part of creator collaborations.

## RhoQ Content Strategy

RhoQ encourages content that is useful, authentic, and related to fitness.

Examples include workout videos, progress updates, exercise demonstrations, fitness routines, training experiences, and motivational posts.

Creators should focus on content that provides value to their audience rather than simply promoting the platform.

## Technical Product Features

RhoQ uses browser technologies to support parts of its workout experience.

The product has explored technologies including FaceMesh, WebGPU, ONNX Runtime Web, WebCodecs, and WebRTC Insertable Streams.

These technologies can support capabilities such as browser-based video processing, workout-related computer vision, and media handling.

The exact technical implementation may change as the product evolves.

## Video Processing

RhoQ can process workout video content in the browser.

The platform has explored browser APIs such as MediaRecorder for recording workout sessions.

Recorded media can be divided into chunks before being uploaded.

Chunk-based uploads can make it easier to handle long workout sessions without requiring one large upload.

## Video Storage

RhoQ has explored object storage for workout videos.

Cloudflare R2 can be used to store video files.

Videos can be uploaded as regular video files rather than requiring server-side HLS packaging.

The platform can then use a CDN to deliver stored video content.

Storage retention policies can be applied to prevent storage costs from growing indefinitely.

## Workout Replay

Recorded workout content can be made available for replay.

The platform can generate previews or lower-resolution representations for situations where a full-quality video is unnecessary.

The goal is to provide useful workout previews while keeping infrastructure costs manageable.

## Infrastructure Philosophy

RhoQ aims to keep infrastructure costs low while the product is still growing.

The platform prefers simple architectures when they provide sufficient functionality.

Managed infrastructure and object storage can be used where they reduce operational complexity.

The system should avoid unnecessary video processing infrastructure when browser-based processing and object storage are sufficient.

## User Experience

RhoQ is designed to make fitness feel less lonely.

The social aspect of seeing other people work out is an important part of the product.

The platform aims to encourage consistency through community participation and accountability.

The experience should feel lightweight and easy to use rather than complicated like a traditional fitness coaching platform.
